How it works

  1. Photograph the date. Get close to the line that says EXP, in good light.
  2. Check what it read. MediCycle shows the date, how sure it is, and lets you fix it.
  3. Keep track. Save it to your cabinet. Opened syrups and drops get their own shorter deadline.

About

MediCycle reads expiry dates from photos using OCR that runs on your device. Photos are not uploaded and your cabinet is saved only in this browser. The Ask tab answers from a small guide with a source for every answer, and it declines dosing and medical questions.

MediCycle does not replace advice from your pharmacist or doctor. Always check the date on the pack yourself.
